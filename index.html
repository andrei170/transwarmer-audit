There is one thing on your homepage that is simply broken, and it is the first finding. It takes minutes to fix and it does not need us.

The foundations are sound

Correct local business markup, nine click-to-call links, GA4 installed and more words on the homepage than most of this trade bothers with. Somebody set this up with some care.

What is wrong is mostly presentation and structure, and one item is a straight bug.

Finding 1 — Your Main Heading Has Two Sentences Stuck Together
THE H1 SERVED ON YOUR HOMEPAGE, 30 SEPTEMBER 2026 "New Warm Roof BenefitsA SupaLite Tiled Roof System will transform your conservatory into a beau..." "Benefits" and "A SupaLite" have run together. There is no space and no break between them. H2 headings on the page: 0
Two headings have collapsed into one

“New Warm Roof Benefits” was meant to be a heading and the SupaLite sentence was meant to be the text underneath it. They have ended up as a single run-on line, which is why it reads oddly.

The H1 is the most important line on any page, both for somebody landing on it and for search engines working out what the page is about. Yours currently reads as a typo.

It is a few minutes in the page editor. Like the rest of Finding 1 in every audit we write, we would rather you knew about it than we saved it for a pitch.

And there are no subheadings at all

936 words with zero H2 headings. Nobody scanning that page can find the TapcoSlate section, the ExtraLight section or the SkyVista glass panels, because there is nothing to scan by.

The content is there and it is decent. It just has no structure over the top of it.

Finding 2 — Somebody On Your Homepage At Ten At Night Has To Remember To Ring You
FORMS ON THE SITE / (homepage) 0 936 words, nine phone links, no form /about-contact-us 1 /customers (Recent Work) 0 Canonical tag on the homepage: MISSING
Nine click-to-call links is genuinely good, and it is not the whole answer

For this trade the phone is right and you have made it easy. Somebody ready to talk can tap and dial from anywhere on the page.

A conservatory roof is rarely a same-day decision. It gets researched in the evening, discussed with a partner, and revisited. That person often is not ready to ring, and on your homepage there is nothing else for them to do.

They have to find the about and contact page to write to you. Most will not bother.

Why this matters more for you than for most

You told Elijah you want your own week down to ten hours. Enquiries that arrive in writing, with the detail already in them, are the ones you can hand to somebody else. Phone calls are the ones that have to come through you.

A form is not just about capturing more. It is about capturing them in a form somebody else can action. That is the thread running through this whole document.

Finding 3 — Nothing Here Is Aimed At The Three People You Want To Hire
WHAT YOU TOLD ELIJAH "doesn't have enough quality staffing behind him to handle more jobs" "staffing are not committed neither got the skillset he needs" "current staffing is 3 to 5, wants to get an extra 3 quality and committed" Goal: reduce his own week from 40 hours to 1-10 hours WHAT THE SITE OFFERS SOMEBODY LOOKING FOR THAT WORK Careers or vacancies page none Anything about the team none found Pay, hours, how to apply nothing
This is the constraint, and there is nothing pointed at it

You have been stuck on this for a few years by your own account, and the website has never addressed it. Every word on it speaks to a homeowner.

A fitter deciding where to work looks a firm up the same way a customer does. A firm with a page that says what the work is, what it pays and who to ring is a rarity in this trade, and it is cheap to build.

The uncomfortable arithmetic, from your own numbers

One to three jobs a week at £10,000 to £12,000 is a real business already. Adding enquiries to a business that cannot fit the ones it has does not get you to a ten hour week. It gets you to a longer one.

We would rather say that on a Sunday morning than sell you a campaign.

Finding 4 — You Have Analytics But You Cannot Tell What Produces A Job
TAGS FOUND Google Analytics 4 G-QXGVQFVB0R present gtag() present Google Ads conversion not found Meta pixel not found Tag Manager not found Conversion events none identified
You are ahead of most of this trade already

GA4 is installed and collecting, which most firms this size have not managed. You can see how many people arrive.

What is missing is the step that turns a visit into a recorded event. With nine phone links and no call tracking, nothing connects a visit to a call to a job. So the question “where did that job come from?” still gets answered by memory.

That is a one-off setup, not a monthly. And it is worth doing before any spending decision, not after.

What We Would Do, In Order
  • Fix the run-on heading and add subheadings. Minutes of work, free, and it does not need us.
  • Put a form on the homepage — not to get more enquiries, but to get them in a written form somebody other than you can pick up.
  • Build the recruitment page. This is the only item on the list aimed at what you actually told us is wrong.
  • Add call tracking and conversion events to the GA4 you already have. One-off, and every later decision depends on it.
  • Add the canonical tag. Small, technical, five minutes.
  • Do not buy lead generation yet. You want fewer hours, not more jobs you personally have to run.
The question for Sunday

If three committed, skilled fitters started next month, what would actually have to change for your week to drop to ten hours?

If the honest answer is that everything still routes through you, then the job is the systems rather than the staffing, and hiring three people would not fix it on its own.

A forty hour week, three staff short, and a homepage whose main heading is missing a space.

Only one of those is fixable this morning. It is still worth fixing.
