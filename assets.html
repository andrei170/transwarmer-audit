You Asked For Your Week Back, Not For More Jobs

Prepared for Andrew Perridge · Sunday 4 October, 9am · AG1 Consulting
Why This Pack Looks Like This
Your stated goal was forty hours down to ten. Everything here serves that

One to three jobs a week at £10,000 to £12,000 is already a real business. Adding enquiries to a business you are trying to step back from makes your week longer, not shorter — so nothing in this pack is a lead generation pitch, and there is no pricing in any of it.

⭐ And you are in better shape than most firms we audit. Three of the four businesses we looked at this week have no measurement at all and no local business markup. You have both. What is wrong is structure, one straight bug, and what happens to an enquiry after it arrives.

Everything In One Place
  • Pre-Call Audit — start here. What is genuinely good, seven problems in order, and seven questions. Finding two is a bug you can have fixed before the call.
  • Forty Hours To Ten — where the week goes, what can come off you without hiring anybody, what cannot, and the order it would have to happen in. ⭐ This is the main conversation.
  • Concept Page — your homepage with a heading that works, subheadings so people can find things, and a form on it. ⚠️ A design proposal, not a live site.
The Short Version
⭐ What is already good — we would not touch it

Correct local business markup with your address, opening hours and coordinates. Nine tap-to-call links. Google Analytics installed. Enquiry forms on your contact page and all three product pages. A ten year guarantee, three clearly differentiated products, and benefits written for the customer rather than the trade.

⭐⭐ And the best line on your website, which is buried near the bottom: “From the first time you contact us, you'll be treated with respect, instead of as a prospect.” ⭐ Thirty-five years of experience gets one clause in the same paragraph. Both deserve to be at the top.

🔴 1. Your main heading is broken — free, fix it this morning

A heading is opened at one level and closed at another, so the paragraph below it gets swallowed into your most important line of text. We verified it in the code. ⚠️ And there are no subheadings at all across nine hundred and seventy words.

🔴 2. The one page without a form is the one everybody lands on — small fix

You have four enquiry forms and none of them is on your homepage or your Recent Work page. Your “Get a Quote!” button does not open a form either — it sends people to another page to go and find one.

🔴 3. Analytics is installed and measuring nothing that matters — already paid for

No conversion tracking, no call tracking, no advertising tag. Four forms and no record of a single submission. ⚠️ So “where did that job come from?” is answered from memory — and memory is exactly what you are trying to take out of the business.

⚠️ 4. Nothing anywhere is aimed at the three people you want — the stated constraint

No careers page, no team page, nothing a fitter would read. And by your own account this has been the position for a few years, which is usually a sign the cause is structural rather than a run of bad hires.

The Question That Decides Everything Else
Are you on the tools? ⭐ If you are fitting roofs for most of the week, then ten hours means somebody else has to fit roofs, and that is a hiring and training problem no system touches. If the hours are really in quoting, surveying, scheduling, chasing and answering that mobile, then ten hours is genuinely reachable — because all of that is process, and process can be moved off a person. Those are two completely different conversations and we would rather know which one we are having in the first two minutes.
