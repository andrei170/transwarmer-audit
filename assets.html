Two Documents, And Neither One Tries To Sell You More Jobs.

Prepared for Andrew Perridge · AG1 Consulting · Sunday 4 October, 9am

You want your own week down from forty hours to ten. That is not a lead generation problem.

One to three jobs a week at £10,000 to £12,000 is already a real business. Adding enquiries to a business that cannot fit the ones it has makes your week longer, not shorter.

Two documents • No pricing in either • Concept page is a proposal, not a live site
Everything In One Place
  • Digital Presence Audit — start here. Eleven items graded and four findings, measured from your own site. Finding 1 is a straight bug you can fix this morning.
  • Concept Page — your homepage with a heading that reads properly, subheadings that let people find things, and a form. A design proposal, not a live site.
The Short Version
1. YOUR MAIN HEADING IS BROKEN fix this morning, free "New Warm Roof BenefitsA SupaLite Tiled Roof System will..." Two sentences run together with no space. And zero H2 subheadings across 936 words. 2. NINE WAYS TO RING YOU, NO WAY TO WRITE no form on the homepage The only form is on the about and contact page. A written enquiry is one somebody else can answer. A phone call is one that has to come through you. 3. NOTHING AIMED AT THE THREE PEOPLE YOU NEED the actual constraint No careers page. No team page. Nothing for a fitter. Stuck on this for a few years, by your own account. 4. GA4 IS INSTALLED BUT MEASURES NOTHING USEFUL No conversion events, no call tracking, no ads tag. "Where did that job come from?" is still answered by memory.
What is already good, and we would not touch

Correct local business markup with your address, hours and coordinates. Nine click-to-call links. 936 words on the homepage, which is more than most of this trade publishes.

The foundations are sound. What is wrong is structure, one bug, and the fact that none of it is pointed at your actual problem.

The question for Sunday

If three committed, skilled fitters started next month, what would have to change for your week to drop to ten hours?

If the honest answer is that quoting, surveying and scheduling all still run through you, then hiring three people does not get you there on its own — and the job is the systems rather than the staffing. Worth working out which it is before anybody spends anything.
